Featured Stories

Choose a story and discover a new world.

Anastasia

Anastasia follows a young woman who sets out on a journey to discover her true identity and uncover the mysteries of her past. Along the way, she meets new friends and faces many challenges while searching for her family and a place she can call home.

Little Red Riding Hood

Little Red Riding Hood is a classic fairy tale about a young girl who travels through the forest to visit her grandmother. Along the way, she encounters a wolf who tries to deceive her. The story teaches the importance of being careful and listening to advice.

Little Women

Little Women follows the four March sisters as they grow up together and experience the challenges of family, friendship, love, and life. Each sister has her own dreams and personality, making their journey a story about family, courage, and growing up.

Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone

Harry Potter discovers that he is a wizard and begins his magical education at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. Together with his new friends, he learns about magic and discovers secrets surrounding his past.

The Land of Stories

The Land of Stories follows twins who discover a magical world filled with fairy-tale characters. They enter this mysterious world and go on an exciting adventure while meeting heroes, villains, and magical creatures from famous stories.

Hansel and Gretel

Hansel and Gretel are siblings who become lost in a mysterious forest and discover a house made of sweets. However, the house hides a dangerous secret. The siblings must use their courage and intelligence to escape and find their way home.
